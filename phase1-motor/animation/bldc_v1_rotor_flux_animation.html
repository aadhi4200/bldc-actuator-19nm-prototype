BLDC v1 (24N28P): how the rotor turns

Each of the 24 stator teeth is an electromagnet. Its polarity follows the three phase currents (winding AabBCcaABbcC × 2, currents timed for maximum torque as in FOC). The pattern stays slightly ahead of the 28 rotor magnets and pulls them around. The currents go through 14 cycles per rotor turn. Simplified: the main magnetic effect only, not a field simulation.
